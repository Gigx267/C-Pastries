Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about ordering, delivery, and more

What types of products do you offer?

+

We offer a variety of fresh baked goods and small chops, including pies, pastries, Chapman, suya wings, puff puff, meat pies, and more. We also have special menu items and seasonal treats depending on availability.

Do you offer pickup or delivery?

+

Yes! We provide both pickup and delivery. Delivery is currently available within a region in the Greater Toronto Area (GTA).

Do you accept bulk or catering orders?

+

Absolutely! We handle bulk and catering orders for birthdays, weddings, corporate events, and other occasions. Please contact us on Instagram or WhatsApp to place large orders or discuss custom requests.

Do your pastries contain allergens?

+

Yes. Our products may contain dairy and eggs. If you have any food allergies or dietary restrictions, please contact us before placing an order.

Do you deliver outside the GTA?

+

At the moment, we only deliver within the Greater Toronto Area (GTA). Customers outside the area are welcome to choose pickup.

How can I place an order?

+

You can place your order directly through our website or we also accept orders through DoorDash.

What is your return/refund policy?

+

Pickup Orders: Due to the perishable nature of our products, we do not accept returns. If there is an issue with your order, please contact us within 24 hours so we can assist you.

Delivery Orders:

  • Incorrect Address: If your order is not received due to an incorrect or incomplete address, we cannot offer a refund or replacement.
  • Failed Delivery Attempts: If the courier attempts delivery multiple times and the order is returned, we cannot provide a refund or replacement.
  • Damaged or Incorrect Orders: If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, please send us a clear photo of the issue so we can assess eligibility for a refund or replacement. Without a photo, we cannot process the claim.

How can I contact customer service?

+

  • Email: cspastries3@gmail.com

What is your pre-order schedule?

+

We accept pre-orders Monday to Thursday until 11:00 PM. All pre-orders are delivered on Saturday within the GTA. Pickup is also available on Saturdays. For bulk, customized, or catering pre-orders, or if you need an order for a different day, please contact us on Instagram or WhatsApp.
